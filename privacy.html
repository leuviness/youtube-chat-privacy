Youtube-chat: privacy policy

Youtube-chat is a personal tool. Only its owner uses it.

What it does

  • It reads the owner's YouTube playlists.
  • It downloads the videos to the owner's computer.
  • It removes downloaded videos from the playlist.

What it does not do

  • It does not send your data to anyone.
  • It does not sell or share any data.
  • It does not keep data on a server.

All data stays on the owner's computer.

You can remove access at any time: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
